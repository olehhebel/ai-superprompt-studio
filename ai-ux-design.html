TRUST IS INTERACTION DESIGN

Users need control at the moments where consequences change.

The right interface does not expose model internals. It gives users enough context to decide: what will happen, what is happening, what happened, and what can be changed.

  • Intent and scope before execution.
  • Progress and tool use during execution.
  • Review and evidence after execution.
  • Undo, correction, retry and escalation when the result is wrong.
