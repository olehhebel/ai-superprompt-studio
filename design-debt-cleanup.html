DESIGN DEBT CLEANUP

Turn “almost the same” UI into one product system.

I identify duplicate patterns, inconsistent states and design-to-code drift, then collapse them into a smaller set of reusable product rules.

WHAT DEBT LOOKS LIKE

The product pays for every rule it cannot reuse.

  • Multiple buttons with the same job but different styles.
  • Modal, table, form and empty-state variants that evolved independently.
  • Spacing and typography that differ by feature team or generated screen.
  • Figma and production components with different names or behaviors.
  • Prompts that must restate the same rules on every task.
