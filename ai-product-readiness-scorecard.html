WHY THESE FIVE LAYERS

Fast-built products usually fail between working and dependable.

AI coding tools reduce implementation time, but they do not automatically create coherent interaction rules, complete states or justified user trust. That is why a working MVP can still feel prototype-like under real use.

The scorecard condenses the same problems covered in the Prototype Debt Map, the Agent Drift Loop and the Autonomy × Reversibility model.

If your lowest section is System, look at the Agent-Ready Design System Sprint. If it is AI Trust or Recovery, look at the AI Agent UX Sprint. If the whole flow still feels unfinished, start with Product Rescue.
